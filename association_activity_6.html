Department of Agricultural and Civil Engineering, Association Activity

15 October 2026

Poetry Writing and Professional Online Presence Development

On 15 October 2026, students from Agricultural and Civil Engineering participated in a session on Poetry Writing and Professional Online Presence Development, combining creative writing with an introduction to open-source contribution and professional digital identity.

  • Introduction to open-source contribution
    Students created GitHub accounts and made their first contributions to the department magazine repository, Eclatineers. Each student submitted their work through a pull request, gaining practical experience with the basic workflow involved in contributing to a shared repository.
  • Acrostic poetry and personal expression
    Students wrote acrostic poems in which the initial letters of the lines form their names. The activity provided an opportunity to explore poetry as a form of personal expression while encouraging students to reflect on their individual qualities, interests, and identities.
  • Poetry reading and presentation
    Several students volunteered to read their poems aloud. The readings gave students an opportunity to present their writing before their peers and experience the transition from written composition to oral performance.

Outcome: The session brought together creative writing, public presentation, and practical digital skills. Students completed an original piece of writing while also gaining their first experience of contributing to a collaborative open-source project.
