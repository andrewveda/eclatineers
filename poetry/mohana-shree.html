An Acrostic Poem

March is the month i despise,

Over and above the cruel summer.

Haunted whispers of her chained me

All when i was still in the cold room

Naive little me thought i was better, but

All i would dream is to be her

Something about her is fascinating.

Her intelligence and disciplined soul

Returns as hatered to my heart.

Everything about her is perfect

Enki, my lord! let me be her, just once.

— Mohana shree, ECE-2, September 10, 2026
